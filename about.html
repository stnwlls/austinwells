Growing up, I was that kid who'd meticulously organize cables behind the entertainment center and press every remote button just to see what happened. Now, I'm a tech enthusiast who lives for video game adventures, beach trips, and discovering the latest EDM artist. Always curious and never afraid to dive deep into how things work, I'm constantly amazed by the rapid pace of technological innovation.

Today, I'm a front-end web developer with a background in luxury hospitality, travel, and hands-on problem solving. I have my Associate of Applied Science in Web Design and Development from South Mountain Community College, where I graduated with a 4.0 GPA, joined Phi Theta Kappa Honor Society, served as President of the Ctrl+Alt+Del Computer Club, was selected for Phi Theta Kappa's 2026 All-USA Academic Team, and was named New Century Workforce Pathway Scholar as Arizona's top workforce-pathway student.

The technologies that excite me most are the ones that turn ideas into useful, polished experiences: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Python, Swift, WordPress, UX design, and the growing role of AI tools in creative and technical workflows. I'm drawn to the details that make digital products feel clear, fast, accessible, and genuinely helpful.
